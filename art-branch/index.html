Soup Pot: art templates

Everything to redraw. You'll draw in the ready-made Aseprite files: download them here (zip). This page shows what each piece is, where it sits and which poses it needs. The full instructions are in the art README.

See your drawings in the game: drop your Aseprite files on the scene preview, which shows them in the kitchens and at the judging table right away.

Only the first frame of each pose is required. Extra frames are optional animation: left empty, the game holds the first frame. Poses marked "also used for" share one drawing, so it's drawn once.
